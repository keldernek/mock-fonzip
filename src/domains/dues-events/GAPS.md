# dues-events gaps

Spec gaps found while implementing the dues-events operations. Use the tables in `../../../PARITY.md` as the format: the spec location, the problem, and what the mock does. Give each waiver in `parity-waivers.json` next to this file a matching row here.

## Membership dues and debts

Dues and debts share one ledger, the `subscriptions` collection. The record's `status` tells them apart: 1, 6 and 8 are debts, and 2, 3, 4 and 7 are payments, cancellations, refunds and balance fixes.

## Simulations

These events happen on the public site or at the payment provider, so `simulations.ts` lets a client trigger them with `POST /__mock/simulations/dues-events/<name>`. Each one writes the real store records, so the list and detail endpoints show the result.

| Simulation               | What it changes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Webhook                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `membership-due-payment` | Records a card payment (status 2) for `user_id`. `amount` defaults to what the member owes. A successful payment settles open debts oldest first and adds to `total_financial`. With `outcome: failed`, the failed transaction appears under `GET /subscriptions?status=failed` and settles nothing.                                                                                                                                | `SUBSCRIPTION` (operation 2), status 3, or 5 when failed, for the payment's id.      |
| `membership-due-refund`  | Refunds a successful payment. Its transaction becomes refunded, a refund record (status 4) joins the ledger, and the member's balance and `total_financial` drop. A second refund gets 409.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | `SUBSCRIPTION`, status 4, for the payment's id.                                      |
| `ticket-sale`            | Sells `quantity` seats of `ticket_id` to a member (`user_id`) or a guest (`name` and `email`), by card or by wire transfer to `bank_account_id`. It follows the ticket's event state, sales window, `max_per_person`, `quantity` (409 when full), `tag_control` and `member_with_no_debt`. `related_with_no_debt` is stored but not enforced, because the mock has no related organisations. A successful sale records a sent mail. | `TICKET` (operation 3), status 3, or 5 for a failed card payment, for the sale's id. |
| `ticket-refund`          | Refunds a successful paid sale. Its seats stop counting as sold. A free sale or a failed sale gets 400, a second refund 409.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | `TICKET`, status 4, for the sale's id.                                               |
