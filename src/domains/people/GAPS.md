### Seed

The newest page is sparse, like production's first page for `order_by=-id`. The 20 newest users (`NEWEST_PAGE` in `src/seed/people.ts`) have no address, city, district or work phone. They mix null and text for birthday, TC kimlik, nationality, country, profile picture, work address and work place, and for two phone and three sms permission dates. The holes follow the user's position in the seed and draw nothing from the RNG, so no other domain's seed data moves. Donations, ticket sales and e-card sales copy a contact's address and place when they are created, so those copies are empty for the same users.

The newest user (id 1150) matches the detail production showed. Its address, city, district, home phone, post code, work phone, work post code, second email and work place are null, and its TC kimlik number, birthday, nationality and permission dates are set. It has one lead (`lead_type` 4, `outcome` 0, no amount) next to its derived events.
